### How to Play (5 Simple Steps)

1. **The Ante:** Place two equal bets in the "Hand 1" and "Hand 2" Ante circles.
2. **The Deal:** The player receives 4 hole cards face-down; the dealer receives 3 hole cards face-down. The Flop (3 community cards) is dealt face-up.
3. **Arrange Your Hands:** Strategy Phase — split your 4 hole cards into two distinct 2-card hands.
4. **Check or Raise:** Check or Raise (2× Ante) each hand independently.
5. **The Showdown:** Turn and River cards are revealed. Each hand is evaluated independently against the dealer’s best 5-card hand.

## 2. Badugi Chase™

**The Baby Rainbow.**

Three cards, then The Chase: pick one community card to finish a four-card Badugi. Charles Chang, co-inventor.

- **Intellectual Property:** Protected by U.S. Patent Nos. 11,810,421, 12,131,608, and U.S. Patent Application 2023/0419777 (Assignee: 2 HH, LLC).

### How to Play (App Version)

1. **Ante & Deal:** Place an Ante bet. Player and dealer each receive 3 cards face-down.
2. **Raise or Fold:** View your hand and choose to Raise (1×, 2×, or 3× Ante) or Fold.
3. **The Chase:** 3 community cards are dealt face-up. Select 1 card to complete your 4-card hand; the remaining 2 cards pass to the dealer.
4. **Showdown:** Dealer reveals their hand. Unmatched suits/ranks form Badugi hands (4-card beats 3-card; lowest top card wins; Aces low).

### How to Play (Live Version)

3. **The Chase:** 3 community cards are dealt face-up. Select 1 card to complete your 4-card hand; discard the remaining 2 cards.
4. **Showdown:** Dealer reveals their hand. Unmatched suits/ranks form Badugi hands (4-card beats 3-card; lowest top card wins; Aces low).
