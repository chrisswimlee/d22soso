# The Living Archive — launch film

**What it is:** A miniature island of Wayne “D22-soso” Chiang’s career, flown like a game and read like a museum.

**Who / what it does:** For anyone who should know the name. It puts the 1999 Brood War title, Cube Draft, 2 Hand Hold’em, the WSOP, and the book in one place you can enter.

**What sets it apart:** Not a résumé. Six sectors, a scout, signs you click.

**Strongest claim:** April 28, 1999, playing Random, he became the first official Brood War world champion.

**Visual hook:** The real trophy portrait lifts, and the island pulls back out of the dark.

**UI shown:** The actual Three.js diorama — signs, scout, minimap — not a redraw.

**Tone:** Cinematic, in the archive’s own voice. Gold on `#07080b`. No trailer shouting.

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, 20.0s.
