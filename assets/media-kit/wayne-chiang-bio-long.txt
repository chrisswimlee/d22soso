Wayne "D22-soso" Chiang — Long bio (for features, podcasts, and press)
=======================================================================

Wayne "D22-soso" Chiang helped define competitive gaming before it had a
mainstream name. Introduced to competitive Magic: The Gathering through Brian
Weissman at Round Table Pizza in Palo Alto, he found his true calling in
real-time strategy. As SoSOWAC—mentored by Case’s Ladder co-owner Bill “Bill1”
Macaitis—he topped Case’s Ladder in Command & Conquer (1996) with Team Synergy
and, in Warcraft II (1997), hit Top 3 in 1v1 and #1 teams with Clan C4L, where
he met Jay “Gadianton” Severson.

When StarCraft released on March 31, 1998, he immediately claimed Case’s Ladder
#1 in 1v1 and teams, won the first StarCraft tournament at Neutral Ground
Mountain View against Severson, maintained Top 20 Battle.net ranks from April
1998 through December 1999, and became StarCraft’s first tournament caster at
PGL Season 3. Recruited by Dennis “Thresh” Fong and Bob “Calbear” Colayco, he
led GX StarCraft on Gamers.com—blogging the international tournaments, battle
reports written immediately after matches analyzing top-tier ladder play, and
Pimpest Plays, the blueprint for modern esports highlight reels.

At the 1999 Brood War Season 1 Ladder Tournament—Blizzard’s first official
Brood War international championship—Chiang played Random. He bested Nautosoft,
Shin Ju Young, and Guillaume “Grrrr…” Patry, then defeated Crexis (Chris Low)
on Showdown for the $2,500 world title. Later that year: PGL Season 4 2nd,
USA vs Canada MVP, Sports Seoul #5, World Champions Invitational 3rd, and
Battle.net World Championships 3rd.

Beyond RTS, he made consistent PTQ Top 8s while Magic was still a side game,
qualified for three Magic Pro Tours (New York 2001, Boston 2002), and is
publicly credited by Brian Weissman and Tom Martell for originating curated
draft pools that became Cube Draft. After 2002 he remained a casual
competitor: Warcraft III US West FFA #1 / Arranged Teams #2, Clash Royale
Challenger, League of Legends Diamond 5, Hearthstone Legend #35 Standard NA
and #11 Wild NA, and #1 in Once Upon a Galaxy.

Today he is Talent Manager for the World Series of Poker, overseeing the
official Vlogger Program, and inventor of patented casino games including
2 Hand Hold’em™ (2015; U.S. Pat. 11,117,045, 11,731,032, 12,005,342) and
Badugi Chase™ with co-inventor Charles Chang. 2 Hand Hold’em is certified
by the Washington State Gambling Commission and live at Great American
Casino Tukwila (Maverick Gaming), with expansion to Los Angeles, San
Antonio, Las Vegas, Silver Dollar Casino SeaTac, and a future Washington
floor. With Fortune 500 executive and WSOP champion Joe Matton he co-authored
Betting on Yourself: Winning in Business, Poker, and the Age of AI
(Kendall Hunt). At the 2025 WSOP he interviewed StarCraft legend
SlayerS_BoxeR.
