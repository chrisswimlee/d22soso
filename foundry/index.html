Sector 04 · Patented table games · 2015 – today

Two hands. One board.

2 Hand Hold’em™ gives the player four hole cards to split into two independent hands against a single dealer board. It’s certified by the Washington State Gambling Commission and dealt live in casinos.

A playable 2 Hand Hold’em demo is in the 3D archive.

How to play 2 Hand Hold’em

  1. The Ante Two equal bets, one for each hand.
  2. The Deal Four hole cards for you, three for the dealer, and a three-card flop.
  3. Arrange Split your four cards into two separate two-card hands.
  4. Check or Raise Check or raise 2× the ante on each hand independently.
  5. Showdown Turn and river come out. Each hand plays the dealer on its own.
