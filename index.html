2 Hand Hold’em™

Twice the Action. Twice the Fun.

U.S. Pat. 11,117,045 · 11,731,032 · 12,005,342 · Assignee 2 HH, LLC

In 2015, Chiang redefined casino Texas Hold’em by giving players dual agency—managing two hands simultaneously against a single dealer board without slowing down pit operations due to simultaneous play.

Certified by the Washington State Gambling Commission.

How to Play — 5 Simple Steps

  1. 01 The Ante — Place two equal bets in the Hand 1 and Hand 2 Ante circles.
  2. 02 The Deal — Player receives 4 hole cards face-down; dealer receives 3 hole cards face-down. The Flop (3 community cards) is dealt face-up.
  3. 03 Arrange Your Hands — Strategy phase: split your 4 hole cards into two distinct 2-card hands.
  4. 04 Check or Raise — Check or Raise (2× Ante) each hand independently.
  5. 05 The Showdown — Turn and River are revealed. Each hand is evaluated independently against the dealer’s best 5-card hand.

Badugi Chase™

The Baby Rainbow.

U.S. Pat. 11,810,421 · 12,131,608 · App. 2023/0419777 · Assignee 2 HH, LLC

Badugi Chase™ bridges the gap between classic draw poker and high-speed casino table games. Designed alongside co-inventor Charles Chang, it introduces The Chase—a communal draft moment of pure tension where a single choice changes the hand.

How to Play

  1. 01 Ante & Deal — Place an Ante bet. Player and dealer each receive 3 cards face-down.
  2. 02 Raise or Fold — View your hand and choose to Raise (1×, 2×, or 3× Ante) or Fold.
  3. 03 The Chase — 3 community cards are dealt face-up. Select 1 card to complete your 4-card hand; the remaining 2 cards pass to the dealer.
  4. 04 Showdown — Dealer reveals their hand. Unmatched suits/ranks form Badugi hands (4-card beats 3-card; lowest top card wins; Aces low).
Live Version ruleset
  1. 01 Ante & Deal — Place an Ante bet. Player receives 3 cards face-down; dealer receives 5 cards face-down.
  2. 02 Raise or Fold — View your hand and choose to Raise (1×, 2×, or 3× Ante) or Fold.
  3. 03 The Chase — 3 community cards are dealt face-up. Select 1 card to complete your 4-card hand; discard the remaining 2 cards.
  4. 04 Showdown — Dealer reveals their hand. Unmatched suits/ranks form Badugi hands (4-card beats 3-card; lowest top card wins; Aces low).

About Wayne Chiang

  • Elite Gamer
  • Game Designer
  • Media Executive
  • Author

Welcome! I’m Wayne Chiang—known in the competitive gaming world as D22-soso.

My career has been defined by a single driving passion: understanding strategy, risk, and human competition, whether on a digital battlefield, at a high-stakes poker table, or in the boardroom. From winning world championships in esports to designing an everlasting Magic: The Gathering format, patenting casino games and producing world-class livestreams, I bridge the gap between competitive gaming culture, game design, and modern media.

Whether it’s managing risk at the table, designing a new game mechanic, or producing high-stakes live media, the goal remains the same: master the system, anticipate the meta, and execute.
Read the full story ▾

Esports & Gaming Pioneer

Before organized leagues, global arenas, or streaming platforms existed, three forces collided—Kali, Case’s Ladder, and real-time strategy—and I competed at the bleeding edge of that frontier, with one foot still in tabletop strategy.

World Champion Status
In 1999, I won the Battle.net Season 1 Ladder World Championship, the first official world title for StarCraft: Brood War (Random). Before that, on Kali and Case’s Ladder—mentored by Bill “Bill1” Macaitis—I held #1 rankings in Command & Conquer with Team Synergy and Top 3 / #1 team ranks in Warcraft II with Clan C4L.
Esports Media & “Pimpest Plays”
With Thresh and Calbear at Gamers.com I ran GX StarCraft—blogging the international tournaments, battle reports written immediately after high-stakes matches analyzing top-tier ladder play, and “Pimpest Plays,” the curated highlight concept that helped define how inventive play was documented for the community.
Magic: The Gathering
Introduced to competitive Magic through Brian Weissman at Round Table Pizza in Palo Alto; consistent PTQ Top 8s while Magic was still a side game; two-time Pro Tour competitor (qualified for three); and the original creator of the Cube Draft format.

Poker, Media & Game Innovation

The strategy, game theory, and risk management skills built in early esports naturally led me into professional poker and gaming development during the 2000s boom.

Livestream & Executive Producer
As co-owner and executive producer of Live at the Bike (LATB), I helped build one of the most iconic cash-game livestream broadcasts out of the Parkwest Bicycle Casino. Today, as Senior Creative Producer and Talent Manager at the World Series of Poker (WSOP), I help shape creator programs, rapid-response shoots, and content integration across the global poker ecosystem.
Casino Game Inventor
Bringing the same mathematical precision, tactical depth, and adrenaline from RTS and Magic: The Gathering into the pit: inventor of 2 Hand Hold’em™ (dual-hand Texas Hold’em, 2015; U.S. Pat. 11,117,045, 11,731,032, 12,005,342) and, with co-inventor Charles Chang, Badugi Chase™ (U.S. Pat. 11,810,421, 12,131,608). Assignee: 2 HH, LLC. Live at Great American Casino Tukwila; expanding to Los Angeles, San Antonio, Las Vegas, Silver Dollar Casino SeaTac, and a future Washington floor.

Education & Strategic Vision

  • B.A. in Economics · UC San Diego · 2007

    My academic background in analytical economics provided the underlying framework for everything that followed—from bankroll management and game theory to media analytics and AI optimization.

  • Professional Program Screenwriting · UC Los Angeles · 2011

    In screenwriting, I was a finalist at Screenplay Expo and Scriptopolooza and interviewed twice for the UCLA Screenwriting MFA before moving on.
