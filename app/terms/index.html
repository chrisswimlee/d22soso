3. Official Rules of Play

By playing 2 Hand Holdem, you agree to abide by the official game rules:

  1. BLINDS: The player posts a blind of equal value on each of the two hands (2 × the chosen bet size) to start the hand.
  2. DEAL: The player receives 4 hole cards face up. 3 community cards ("the flop") are dealt face up on the table. The dealer receives 3 hole cards face down until showdown.
  3. ARRANGE: The player splits the 4 hole cards into two 2-card Hold'em hands (Hand 1 and Hand 2). Cards stay face up while arranging so they can be read and moved. The arrangement locks when the player taps Deal.
  4. RAISE: After seeing the flop and the arranged hands, the player may Check (no extra chips) or Raise 2× the blind on each hand individually. Raise is flop-only. There is no fold. Deal locks the split and any raises together and deducts the extra chips.
  5. SHOWDOWN: The turn and river are dealt together, face up. The dealer reveals their 3 hole cards and uses at most 2 of those 3 (combined with the 5 community cards) to form one 5-card poker hand. That hand is compared separately to Hand 1 and Hand 2. A win returns 2× the bet on that hand (even money). A push returns the bet. A loss returns nothing.
